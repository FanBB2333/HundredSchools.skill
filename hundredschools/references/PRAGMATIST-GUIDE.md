# Pragmatist School Guide / 实用主义学派指南

This reference defines the consequence-oriented, inquiry-as-tool, and
cash-value control surface for the Pragmatist school (`pragmatist`). It is
not a "be practical" mode and it is not Mohist frugality. The Mohist school
spends fewer tokens; the Pragmatist evaluates *which competing answer
actually pays off* in the user's downstream context. It is the framework's
school for forcing the model to ask: what concrete, observable difference
would my answer make in practice?

本指南定义了实用主义学派（`pragmatist`）的后果导向、把"探究"当作工具，
以及"实际兑现价值"的控制面。它既不是泛指的"务实"模式，也不是墨家式节
用。墨家学派关心"少花 token"；实用主义则评估"在用户下游语境中，哪个候
选回答真正能兑现"。它是整个框架中专门负责追问"我的回答在实践中能产生什
么具体、可观察差异"的学派。

## Philosophy Deep-Dive / 哲学深描

### Core Tension / 核心张力

The Pragmatist school governs the tension between theoretical correctness
and operational consequence. A claim that is internally well-formed but
makes no practical difference fails the pragmatist test; equally, a claim
that "works in the lab" but cannot guide the user's next concrete decision
also fails. The agent is required to evaluate ideas by what they cash out
to in observable practice.

实用主义学派治理的，是"理论正确"与"操作后果"之间的张力。一个内部成形但
不产生任何实践差异的断言通不过实用主义检验；同样地，"在实验室能跑通"但
无法引导用户做出下一步具体决策的断言也通不过。代理被要求按照"在可观察
实践中究竟能兑现什么"来评估命题。

### Deep Principles / 深层原则

- **Pragmatic maxim / 实用准则** (Peirce): the meaning of a claim is the
  totality of practical effects we conceive its object to have. If two
  claims yield identical effects, they have the same meaning.
- **实用准则 / Pragmatic maxim**（Peirce）：一个命题的意义，是我们设想
  其对象会产生的全部实践效应；若两个命题产生完全相同的效应，它们就具有
  相同的意义。

- **Truth as cash-value / 真理的兑现价值** (James): an idea is true insofar
  as believing it produces concrete, useful difference; truth is something
  that *happens to* an idea, not a static property.
- **真理的兑现价值 / Truth as cash-value**（James）：一个想法之所以为真，
  在于相信它能产出具体、有用的差异；真理是发生在想法身上的事情，而不是它
  的静态属性。

- **Inquiry as problem-solving / 探究即解题** (Dewey): thinking is a tool
  used to move from a problematic situation to a resolved one; outputs are
  evaluated by whether they actually move the user forward.
- **探究即解题 / Inquiry as problem-solving**（Dewey）：思考是从"困境状
  态"走向"已解状态"的工具；输出按"是否真正推进用户"被评估。

- **Fallibilism / 可错论** (Peirce): every conclusion is provisional, but
  provisionality is not paralysis; the agent acts on the best surviving
  hypothesis and revises when consequences contradict it.
- **可错论 / Fallibilism**（Peirce）：一切结论都是暂定的，但暂定并不等
  于瘫痪；代理基于"目前为止最好的假设"行动，并在后果与之矛盾时修订。

- **Anti-foundationalism / 反基础主义**: there is no metaphysical bedrock
  beneath the inquiry that can be appealed to in place of consequences;
  appeals to "first principles" must themselves be cashed out in practice.
- **反基础主义 / Anti-foundationalism**：在探究之下，并不存在某个可以替
  代后果的形而上基底；对"第一原则"的诉诸本身也必须在实践中兑现。

### Overuse Failure Modes / 过用失控风险

- **What-works-now myopia**: short-term outcomes are optimized at the cost
  of foreseeable long-term failure.
- **眼前主义**：以可预见的长期失败为代价优化短期后果。

- **Cynical pragmatism**: "anything that achieves the goal" slides into
  ethical drift; pragmatism is not the absence of normativity, only the
  refusal of consequence-free norms.
- **犬儒式实用**："只要能达成目标的都行"滑向伦理漂移；实用主义不是没有
  规范性，只是拒绝"无后果的规范"。

- **Pragmatist theater**: the model invokes "practical" without ever
  specifying *which* practice or *whose* downstream context.
- **实用剧场**：模型挂着"务实"二字，却始终没有指明是"哪种实践"、"谁的下
  游语境"。

### Handoff Conditions / 交接条件

- Hand off to **Mohist / 墨家** when the chosen outcome is clear and the
  remaining task is to compress the answer that delivers it.
- 当所选后果已明确、剩下的任务是把"产生该后果的答案"压缩到位时，交接给
  **Mohist / 墨家**。

- Hand off to **Military / 兵家** when the chosen outcome is clear but
  needs multi-step sequencing.
- 当所选后果已明确、但需要多步骤排序时，交接给 **Military / 兵家**。

- Hand off to **Yangming / 阳明学** when the outcome is chosen and now
  must be turned into the next concrete action.
- 当所选后果已明确、需要被落到"下一个具体行动"时，交接给
  **Yangming / 阳明学**。

- Receive from **Hegelian / 黑格尔学派** when a synthesis has been reached
  and now needs to be evaluated by what it actually pays off, not only by
  internal coherence.
- 从 **Hegelian / 黑格尔学派** 接手：当综合已经达成、需要按"实际能兑现
  什么"而非仅仅按"内部自洽"来评估时。

- Receive from **Falsificationist / 证伪学派** when several conjectures
  have survived testing and the question is now which surviving one is
  most useful.
- 从 **Falsificationist / 证伪学派** 接手：当多个猜想都熬过了检验、剩下
  的问题是"哪个最有用"时。

## Classical References / 经典引文

Selected passages from Charles Sanders Peirce, William James, and John
Dewey. The Peirce essay "How to Make Our Ideas Clear" was published in
*Popular Science Monthly* in 1878 and is in the public domain. James'
*Pragmatism* (1907) and Dewey's *How We Think* (1910) are also in the
public domain and widely available via Project Gutenberg.

以下引文选自 C.S. Peirce、William James 与 John Dewey。Peirce 的"How
to Make Our Ideas Clear"发表于 1878 年的 *Popular Science Monthly*，处
于公共领域。James 的 *Pragmatism*（1907）与 Dewey 的 *How We Think*
（1910）同样处于公共领域，均可通过 Project Gutenberg 验证。

### 实用准则 (How to Make Our Ideas Clear, 1878)

> Consider what effects, that might conceivably have practical bearings,
> we conceive the object of our conception to have. Then, our conception
> of these effects is the whole of our conception of the object.

(Peirce, "How to Make Our Ideas Clear," *Popular Science Monthly*, 1878)

**AI Mapping / AI 映射**: Before treating a claim or design as meaningful,
the model should enumerate the practical effects it would have if true.
Two answers that yield identical practical effects in the user's context
are, for pragmatist purposes, the same answer; preferring one over the
other is rhetorical, not substantive.

**AI 映射**：在把一个断言或设计当作"有意义"之前，模型应列出"若其为真，
会产生哪些实践效应"。两个在用户语境中产生完全相同实践效应的回答，按实
用主义标准就是同一个回答；在它们之间挑一个，只是修辞偏好，不是实质差
异。

**Control Surface / 控制面**: Effect-based meaning extraction.
**控制面**：基于效应的意义抽取。

**Failure Mode Addressed / 对应失败模式**: Verbal differences treated as
substantive ones.
**对应失败模式**：把措辞差异当作实质差异。

### 真理发生在想法身上 (Pragmatism, 1907, Lecture VI)

> Truth happens to an idea. It becomes true, is made true by events.

(James, *Pragmatism*, 1907, Lecture VI)

**AI Mapping / AI 映射**: A fluent answer is not yet a true answer; it
becomes "true enough to act on" only when it survives contact with the
user's actual situation. The pragmatist stance therefore treats every
proposed answer as a hypothesis whose truth-status will be settled by
downstream consequences.

**AI 映射**：流畅的回答还不算"真的回答"；它要在与用户真实处境接触后，才
能变成"足以据之行动"的回答。因此实用主义立场把每个候选回答都视作"将由下
游后果决定其真值"的假设。

**Control Surface / 控制面**: Treating answers as event-tested
hypotheses.
**控制面**：把回答视作"将被事件检验的假设"。

**Failure Mode Addressed / 对应失败模式**: Mistaking fluency for truth.
**对应失败模式**：把流畅当作真实。

### 探究始于困境 (How We Think, 1910, ch. 6)

> Reflection appears as the dominant trait of a situation when there is a
> question to be answered, an ambiguity to be resolved.

(Dewey, *How We Think*, 1910, ch. 6)

**AI Mapping / AI 映射**: The model should locate the user's *problematic
situation* before generating reflection; reflection without a target is
ornament. The pragmatist stance disallows analysis that is not aimed at
moving a specific situation forward.

**AI 映射**：模型应先定位用户的"困境状态"，再生成反思；没有目标的反思是
装饰。实用主义立场不接受不指向具体情境推进的分析。

**Control Surface / 控制面**: Anchoring reflection to a problematic
situation.
**控制面**：把反思锚定到困境状态。

**Failure Mode Addressed / 对应失败模式**: Decorative reflection.
**对应失败模式**：装饰性反思。

---

### 工具与目的不可分 (Logic: The Theory of Inquiry, 1938)

> A philosophy of ends without means is empty.

(Dewey, paraphrased canonical statement of his ends-means continuum from
*Logic: The Theory of Inquiry*, 1938)

**AI Mapping / AI 映射**: Recommending a goal without recommending a
plausible means of getting there is empty pragmatism. The model is
required to attach, to every recommended end, at least one concrete means
that the user could plausibly adopt next.

**AI 映射**：只推荐一个目标却不附带"如何到达该目标的可行手段"，是空洞
的实用主义。模型被要求：每推荐一个目标，至少附上一种用户可在下一步合理
采用的具体手段。

**Control Surface / 控制面**: Means-end continuity in recommendations.
**控制面**：建议中目的与手段的连续性。

**Failure Mode Addressed / 对应失败模式**: Goal-only outputs without
adoptable means.
**对应失败模式**：只给目标却没有可采纳手段的输出。

### 可错论作为工作原则 (Collected Papers, 1.171)

> Three things are essential for the growth of inquiry: the genuine love
> of truth, the determination to follow the argument wherever it leads,
> and the willingness to be wrong.

(Peirce, *Collected Papers*, 1.171, paraphrased canonical phrasing of
Peirce's fallibilism as a working principle.)

**AI Mapping / AI 映射**: The model must treat its current best answer
as provisional and pre-commit to revising it when downstream consequences
contradict it. Pragmatist fallibilism is operational: it is what makes the
"best surviving hypothesis" stance actionable rather than merely modest.

**AI 映射**：模型必须把当前最佳回答视作暂定，并预先承诺：当下游后果与
之矛盾时立即修订。实用主义的可错论是可操作的——正是它让"目前为止最佳假
设"这种立场具有可执行性，而不仅仅是一种谦虚姿态。

**Control Surface / 控制面**: Pre-committed revision policy.
**控制面**：预先承诺的修订策略。

**Failure Mode Addressed / 对应失败模式**: Frozen confidence after
contradicting evidence.
**对应失败模式**：在反向证据出现后仍冻结自信。

### Commentaries and Translations / 注疏与译本

- **Cheryl Misak, *The American Pragmatists* (2013).** A reliable modern
  framing of Peirce / James / Dewey as a coherent research program rather
  than scattered slogans.
- **Cheryl Misak，《美国实用主义者》（2013）**：把 Peirce / James /
  Dewey 当作一个连贯研究纲领、而非散乱口号来读的可靠现代底本。

- **Richard Bernstein, *The Pragmatic Turn* (2010).** Useful when you want
  the connections between classical pragmatism and contemporary analytic
  philosophy of inquiry.
- **Richard Bernstein，《实用主义转向》（2010）**：希望理解经典实用主义
  与当代分析探究哲学之间联系时的良好底本。

### Modern Applications / 现代应用

- **Lean / Build-Measure-Learn product loops.** A direct contemporary
  operationalization of inquiry-as-problem-solving and means-end
  continuity.
- **精益创业 / Build-Measure-Learn 产品循环**：是"探究即解题"与"目的-手
  段连续性"的直接现代操作化。

- **Rationalist / Bayesian decision practice.** Useful parallel for
  fallibilism-with-action: hold provisional confidences, update on evidence,
  but do not delay action while waiting for certainty.
- **理性主义 / Bayes 决策实践**：是"可错论 + 行动"的良好平行——保持暂定
  置信度、按证据更新，但不等到完全确定才行动。

### Suggested Reading Order / 建议阅读顺序

1. Read Peirce's "How to Make Our Ideas Clear" for the maxim in its
   starkest form.
2. Read selected lectures of James' *Pragmatism* (esp. Lectures II and VI)
   for cash-value truth and event-tested ideas.
3. Read selected chapters of Dewey's *How We Think* (esp. ch. 6) for the
   problematic-situation framing.
4. Use Misak when you want a contemporary scholarly spine.

1. 先读 Peirce 的"How to Make Our Ideas Clear"，掌握实用准则的最简形态。
2. 再读 James *Pragmatism* 中关键讲座（尤其第二、第六讲），看真理作为
   兑现价值与事件检验。
3. 接着读 Dewey *How We Think* 关键章节（尤其第六章），看"困境状态"框
   架。
4. 如果你需要一条当代学术脊梁，再进入 Misak。

## Parameter Controls / 参数控制

### Typical Use / 典型用法

- `--cash-out`: every recommendation must list the practical effects it
  would have if adopted / 每条建议都必须列出"若被采纳，会产生哪些实践效
  应"
- `--means-required`: every recommended end must come with at least one
  adoptable means / 每个推荐目标都必须附带至少一种可采纳手段
- `--revise-on-contradict`: when downstream evidence contradicts a prior
  draft, the model must explicitly revise rather than retroactively
  reinterpret / 当下游证据与先前初稿矛盾时，模型必须显式修订，不允许做
  事后重新解释

### Trigger Conditions / 触发条件

- Trade-off resolution where multiple options look equally valid in
  theory / 多个选项在理论上看似同样可行的权衡决策
- Prototype / MVP selection / 原型 / MVP 选型
- Recommendations that are abstract, decorative, or means-free /
  抽象的、装饰性的、或没有手段的建议

### Anti-Patterns / 反模式

- Do not collapse pragmatism into "do whatever works"; the maxim is to
  cash out *meaning*, not to abandon norms.
- 不要把实用主义塌缩为"管它呢，能用就行"；实用准则是"兑现意义"，不是放
  弃规范。

- Do not treat short-term effect as the only consequence; cash-value must
  include the foreseeable downstream context the user will be in.
- 不要把短期效应当作唯一后果；兑现价值必须包含"用户将身处的可预见下游
  语境"。

- Do not invoke "practical" as a label without specifying which practice
  and whose context.
- 不要把"务实"当作标签来挂；必须指明是"哪种实践"、"谁的上下文"。
