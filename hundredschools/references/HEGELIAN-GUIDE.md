# Hegelian School Guide / 黑格尔学派指南

This reference defines the dialectical, counter-position-generating, and
sublation-oriented control surface for the Hegelian school (`hegelian`). It
is not a "balanced view" mode and it is not generic skepticism. It is the
framework's school for forcing the model to author the strongest possible
counter-case to its own draft, and only then to commit — not to a middle
ground, but to a position that has absorbed what was true in both sides.

本指南定义了黑格尔学派（`hegelian`）的辩证式、反命题生成与"扬弃式综合"
控制面。它既不是"折中观点"模式，也不是泛化的怀疑论，而是整个框架中专门
负责让模型为自己的初稿作出最强反案、并且不止步于"折中"，而是承接两边各
自之"真"后做出一次确定推进的学派。

## Philosophy Deep-Dive / 哲学深描

### Core Tension / 核心张力

The Hegelian school governs the tension between commitment and contradiction.
Modern agents prefer to either commit too early (skipping the strongest
counter-argument) or to remain perpetually neutral (laundering indecision as
"balance"). The dialectical stance refuses both: it commits, but only after
the strongest negation has been authored and answered.

黑格尔学派治理的，是"承诺"与"矛盾"之间的张力。当代代理倾向于要么过早承
诺（跳过最强反论），要么永远中立（把犹豫包装成"平衡"）。辩证立场两者都
拒绝——它会承诺，但只能在最强反论被写出来并被回应之后。

### Deep Principles / 深层原则

- **Aufhebung / 扬弃**: a real synthesis preserves what was true in both
  thesis and antithesis while transcending the form of their opposition; it
  does not merely split the difference.
- **扬弃 / Aufhebung**：真正的综合保留正题与反题各自之"真"，并扬弃二者
  对立的形式；它不是简单的取中。

- **Determinate negation / 规定的否定**: the negation must point at a
  specific load-bearing premise of the thesis, not at the thesis as a whole;
  generic "well, on the other hand" does not count.
- **规定的否定 / Determinate negation**：反论必须指向正题中某个具体承重
  前提，而不是泛泛地反对整个正题；笼统的"另一方面…"不算。

- **The truth is the whole / 真理是整体**: a partial truth held in
  isolation will mislead; the position must be evaluated against its
  integration with what surrounds it.
- **真理是整体 / The truth is the whole**：孤立成立的"局部真理"会导致误
  导；必须把命题与其语境放在一起评估。

- **Concrete universal / 具体共相**: a synthesis must work on the specific
  case at hand, not only at the level of slogan; if it cannot survive a
  concrete instance, it is not yet a synthesis.
- **具体共相 / Concrete universal**：综合必须在具体案例上成立，而不是只
  在口号层面成立；若它通不过一个具体实例，那它还不算综合。

- **History as work / 历史即工作**: the dialectic is not a magic triadic
  template; it is what happens when contradictions are taken seriously and
  worked through rather than suppressed.
- **历史即工作 / History as work**：辩证不是某种神奇的"三段式"模板，而
  是在矛盾被认真对待、被推进而非被压平时所发生的事情。

### Overuse Failure Modes / 过用失控风险

- **Synthesis theater**: the model produces a fake middle ground that
  pleases nobody and resolves nothing.
- **综合剧场**：模型造出一个谁也不满意、什么都没解决的伪中间立场。

- **Triadic compulsion**: the model forces a thesis–antithesis–synthesis
  triad where one side is just wrong and should be rejected outright.
- **三段式强迫症**：当其中一方根本就是错的、应被直接驳回时，模型仍要硬
  凑出三段式综合。

- **Hegelese**: the output is dressed in dialectical vocabulary but the
  contradictions it claims to resolve were never load-bearing.
- **伪黑话**：输出披着辩证词汇，但所声称要"解决"的矛盾根本不是承重矛盾。

### Handoff Conditions / 交接条件

- Hand off to **Military / 兵家** when the synthesis has been chosen and
  the remaining task is sequencing its execution.
- 当综合已经选定、剩下的任务是把它的执行步骤排出来时，交接给
  **Military / 兵家**。

- Hand off to **Legal / 法家** when the synthesis must be locked into a
  structural contract.
- 当综合需要被锁入结构契约时，交接给 **Legal / 法家**。

- Hand off to **Falsificationist / 证伪学派** when the synthesis is in hand
  but its empirical risk has not yet been stated.
- 当综合已经成立、但其经验风险尚未被显式陈述时，交接给
  **Falsificationist / 证伪学派**。

- Receive from **Dao / 道家** when exploration has produced multiple live
  perspectives that now need to be staged as thesis–antithesis pairs and
  worked through.
- 从 **Dao / 道家** 接手：当探索已经产出多个仍然存活的视角、需要被组织
  为正反命题对并被推进时。

### 真理是整体 (Phenomenology of Spirit / 精神现象学, Preface)

> Das Wahre ist das Ganze.

*"The true is the whole."* (Baillie, 1910, *Phenomenology of Spirit*,
Preface)

**AI Mapping / AI 映射**: A claim that is locally correct but isolated from
its surroundings will often mislead. The Hegelian stance forbids reading a
single proposition as adequate; it requires checking the proposition against
its containing context, including the position it would have had to defeat
in order to count as decided.

**AI 映射**：一个局部正确但脱离上下文的命题，往往会误导。黑格尔立场禁
止把单一命题视为充分；它要求把命题放回所属语境中验证，包括它若想被算作
"已决"，本应击败哪个对立命题。

**Control Surface / 控制面**: Whole-context evaluation before commitment.
**控制面**：承诺前的整体性评估。

**Failure Mode Addressed / 对应失败模式**: Locally true but globally
misleading claims.
**对应失败模式**：局部为真却在全局上误导的断言。

### 矛盾是推动力 (Science of Logic / 逻辑学, Doctrine of Essence)

> Der Widerspruch ist die Wurzel aller Bewegung und Lebendigkeit.

*"Contradiction is the root of all movement and vitality."* (Hegel, *Science
of Logic*, Doctrine of Essence; canonical short phrasing of Hegel's claim
that contradiction drives development. English is widely cited via the
A.V. Miller translation, 1969, but the short German is the stable
reference.)

**AI Mapping / AI 映射**: A draft that hides its tensions does not yet
qualify as a finished position. The Hegelian stance treats unsuppressed
contradictions as productive; the model is required to surface them and then
make a determinate move, not to paper over them.

**AI 映射**：把张力藏起来的初稿，尚不算成形的立场。黑格尔立场把"未被压平
的矛盾"视为有产出，并要求模型把矛盾翻到台面上，再做一次确定动作，而不是
把矛盾抹平。

**Control Surface / 控制面**: Surfacing rather than suppressing
contradictions.
**控制面**：把矛盾浮上来，而不是压下去。

**Failure Mode Addressed / 对应失败模式**: Smoothed-over output that hides
its real disagreements.
**对应失败模式**：把分歧藏起来的"看起来一致"的输出。

---

### 规定的否定 (Phenomenology of Spirit / 精神现象学, Introduction)

> die bestimmte Negation

*"determinate negation"* (Baillie, 1910, *Phenomenology of Spirit*,
Introduction)

**AI Mapping / AI 映射**: Generic skepticism does not move a problem
forward; what does is naming the specific load-bearing premise that, if
true, would render the thesis untenable. Determinate negation is the only
form of dissent the Hegelian stance accepts as productive.

**AI 映射**：泛化的怀疑论并不能推进问题；能推进问题的是——指名道姓地说
出某个具体承重前提，使正题如果接受该前提就站不住脚。规定的否定，是黑格
尔立场唯一接受为"有产出"的反论形态。

**Control Surface / 控制面**: Forcing dissent to be specific and
load-bearing.
**控制面**：迫使反论必须具体、必须承重。

**Failure Mode Addressed / 对应失败模式**: Lazy "on the other hand"
dissent.
**对应失败模式**：懒散的"另一方面…"式反论。

### 扬弃保留并超越 (Science of Logic / 逻辑学, Doctrine of Being)

> aufheben

*"to sublate" — to negate, to preserve, and to lift to a higher
determinateness, all at once.* (Hegel, *Science of Logic*, Doctrine of
Being; canonical English translation of the verb *aufheben* used across
Miller 1969 and earlier renderings.)

**AI Mapping / AI 映射**: A real synthesis is not an averaging of two
positions; it preserves what each side got right, drops what was load-bearing
in their opposition, and produces a more determinate position than either
had alone. The Hegelian stance reserves the word "synthesis" for outputs
that pass this test.

**AI 映射**：真正的综合不是两个立场的折中；它保留双方各自之"对"，丢掉
双方对立中的承重错误，并产出比任何一方单独都更确定的立场。黑格尔立场只
把通过这个检验的输出称为"综合"。

**Control Surface / 控制面**: Discipline of what counts as synthesis.
**控制面**：何为"真正综合"的纪律。

**Failure Mode Addressed / 对应失败模式**: Calling an average a synthesis.
**对应失败模式**：把"取平均"称为"综合"。

---

### 现实与理性 (Philosophy of Right / 法哲学原理, Preface)

> Was vernünftig ist, das ist wirklich; und was wirklich ist, das ist
> vernünftig.

*"What is rational is actual; and what is actual is rational."* (Dyde,
1896, *Philosophy of Right*, Preface)

**AI Mapping / AI 映射**: A position is not finished merely by being
internally consistent; it must also be tested against the actual situation
the user is in. A "rational" recommendation that is unworkable in the user's
real context is not yet rational in the sense the Hegelian stance demands.

**AI 映射**：一个立场仅仅"内部自洽"还不算成形；它还必须在用户真实处境
里受检。一个在用户真实情境下不可行的"看似合理"建议，按黑格尔立场的标准
还算不上"真合理"。

**Control Surface / 控制面**: Anchoring abstract reasoning to the user's
actuality.
**控制面**：把抽象推理锚定回用户的真实处境。

**Failure Mode Addressed / 对应失败模式**: Internally tidy but
context-blind recommendations.
**对应失败模式**：内部漂亮但忽视上下文的建议。

### Modern Applications / 现代应用

- **Adversarial review / red-team practices in software and policy.** A
  contemporary operationalization of "author the strongest counter-case
  before committing."
- **软件与政策中的对抗式评审 / red-team 实践**：是"承诺前先写最强反案"
  这一原则的当代操作化。

- **Conflict mediation literature.** Useful parallel for what *Aufhebung*
  looks like outside formal logic: not splitting the difference, but
  re-framing what the parties were really arguing about.
- **冲突调解相关文献**：是"扬弃"在形式逻辑之外的良好平行——不是简单折
  中，而是重新定位双方到底在争什么。

### Suggested Reading Order / 建议阅读顺序

1. Read the Preface and Introduction of *Phenomenology of Spirit* for the
   working stance: truth-as-whole and determinate negation.
2. Read selected sections of *Science of Logic* (Doctrine of Being and
   Doctrine of Essence) for *Aufhebung* and contradiction-as-motor.
3. Read the Preface of *Philosophy of Right* to see how the dialectic
   demands actuality, not only consistency.
4. Use Pippin or other modern commentary if you want method-only without
   metaphysical commitments.

1. 先读《精神现象学》序言与导论，把握工作姿态：真理-整体关系与规定的否
   定。
2. 再读《逻辑学》（存在论与本质论相关章节），理解扬弃与矛盾推动力。
3. 接着读《法哲学原理》序言，看辩证如何要求"现实性"而不只是"自洽"。
4. 如果你只想要方法论而不想承担形而上学承诺，再读 Pippin 等现代注释。

## Parameter Controls / 参数控制

### Typical Use / 典型用法

- `--dialectic-rounds 1`: emit thesis, then a determinate negation, then a
  synthesis once / 输出正题、规定否定，再输出一次综合
- `--dialectic-rounds 2`: run the cycle twice; the second round tests the
  first synthesis / 运行两轮；第二轮检验第一轮的综合
- `--strict-aufhebung`: reject any "synthesis" that is merely an averaging
  of the two prior positions / 拒绝那种只是把双方"取平均"的"综合"

### Trigger Conditions / 触发条件

- Stakeholder disagreement with both sides holding load-bearing claims /
  各方都持有承重断言的利益相关者分歧
- Agent is about to commit on a draft that has not yet faced its strongest
  counter-case / 代理即将承诺一个尚未直面最强反案的初稿
- High-stakes recommendations where averaging would be dishonest / 取中将
  构成"不诚实"的高风险建议

### Anti-Patterns / 反模式

- Do not run Hegelian mode where one side is simply wrong; force-fitting
  triads into asymmetric problems is a failure mode, not a virtue.
- 不要在"明显有一方错"的问题上跑黑格尔模式；把三段式硬塞进非对称问题里
  是失败模式，不是优点。

- Do not treat "synthesis" as a tag attached to an averaging output;
  *Aufhebung* requires preserving what each side got right while transcending
  the form of their opposition.
- 不要把"综合"当作贴在折中输出上的标签；扬弃要求保留各方之"对"并扬弃二
  者对立的形式。

- Do not use Hegelian mode in a step where the user explicitly needs a
  short, decisive answer; hand off to Mohist or Yangming for compression
  and action.
- 不要在用户明确要求"短而决定性回答"的步骤上使用黑格尔模式；交给墨家或
  阳明学做压缩与行动化。
